Advice Before a Project Is Needed

Not every quality, compliance, or technology challenge requires a formal consulting project. Sometimes an experienced, independent perspective can help clarify the situation, evaluate risk, and identify the appropriate path forward.

The CoreGxP Advisory Partnership provides ongoing access to experienced quality, compliance, regulatory, and technology guidance for organizations operating in regulated life sciences environments.

The relationship is designed for questions and emerging issues that arise between formal projects, giving leadership, Quality, technology, and operational teams access to an experienced advisor before uncertainty develops into a larger problem.

How the Advisory Partnership Can Help

  • Emerging quality, compliance, and data integrity concerns
  • Technology and vendor decisions
  • Computerized systems and validation strategy
  • Regulatory and inspection-related questions
  • Review of proposed corrective actions and risk mitigation
  • Independent perspective on complex or cross-functional challenges
  • A sounding board for Quality, technology, operational, and business leadership

When to Use the Advisory Partnership

The Advisory Partnership is intended for situations where questions arise, risks are beginning to emerge, or an independent perspective would help inform a decision before a larger engagement is necessary.

Before a Technology Decision

Evaluate regulatory, quality, data integrity, validation, and operational considerations before committing to a technology, vendor, or implementation approach.

When a Compliance Concern Emerges

Develop a clearer understanding of the situation, assess potential risk, and consider practical next steps before the issue becomes more difficult to manage.

When the Answer Isn't Obvious

Bring an independent perspective to complex situations involving multiple stakeholders, competing priorities, or uncertainty about regulatory expectations.

Advisory Support or a Defined Engagement?

The Advisory Partnership is intended to provide experienced perspective and guidance without requiring a new consulting project every time a question arises.

When a matter requires a formal audit, assessment, remediation effort, validation activity, investigation, or other substantial deliverable, the work can be separately scoped as a CoreGxP consulting engagement.
